## Groups

### 24. A calendar for the whole group
![Group in real time](gifs/24-grupo-en-tiempo-real.gif)

Any number of people share one canvas. Each member gets a colour, a cursor with their name (and a "writing…" indicator), and a place in the avatar stack on the top bar; click an avatar to follow that person's camera. Filter chips with live counters show one person's plans or everyone's. See [GROUPS.md](GROUPS.md) for invites and scaling. *(This clip simulates three members connecting; the real multi-tab behaviour is covered by the demo mode.)*

## Canvas & navigation

### 1. Infinite zoom
![Infinite zoom](gifs/01-zoom-infinito.gif)

Zoom from the whole year down to a single day on one continuous canvas. Three levels of detail keep it readable at every scale: month names become posters when zoomed out, and every plan is expanded when zoomed in. The camera is hand-written (inertia, pinch, smooth zoom flights) and holds 60 fps.

### 2. Navigation shortcuts
![Navigation shortcuts](gifs/02-atajos-de-navegacion.gif)

One click flies the camera to **this week** or to the **next long weekend**, always framed so the toolbar never covers the destination. Month and week dropdowns, a date-range picker and keyboard shortcuts cover the rest.

### 23. Minimap
![Minimap](gifs/23-minimapa.gif)

A minimap shows where you are on the canvas and lets you jump anywhere in one click.

## Free-form layout

### 3. Move months anywhere
![Move months](gifs/03-mover-meses.gif)

Each month is an object. Drag it by its title to arrange the calendar however your head works — not in the order a calendar app dictates.

### 4. Your view ↔ calendar view
![Your view versus calendar view](gifs/04-vista-calendario-vs-libre.gif)

Toggle between your own custom arrangement and a tidy 4-column calendar grid with a single button (or `V`). Months animate smoothly between the two layouts and your custom positions are never lost.

### 5. Ungroup a month
![Ungroup months](gifs/05-desagrupar-meses.gif)

Ungroup a month to move each of its days independently. A halo keeps the month visually together and follows its days wherever they go. Regroup at any time.

## Resizing days

### 6. Resize a day to see its hourly grid
![Resize a day](gifs/06-redimensionar-dia-y-horarios.gif)

Drag a day's corner to enlarge it and it turns into a full hourly timeline. Timed plans sit on their hours, and plans that overlap are placed side by side automatically.

### 7. Days that grow with their content
![Days grow automatically](gifs/07-dias-que-crecen-solos.gif)

Days size themselves to how many plans they hold, so a busy day is always readable without manual tweaking.

### 8. Reset sizes
![Reset sizes](gifs/08-restablecer-tamanos.gif)

One button returns every day of a month to its automatic size.

## Connections & loose plans

### 9. Arrows
![Arrows](gifs/09-flechas.gif)

Connect anything — months, days or individual plans — with an arrow, and give it a label. Arrows are fully curved and route around other months instead of cutting through them.

### 10. Shape the curve
![Shape the arrow curve](gifs/10-curva-moldeable.gif)

Drag the handle on an arrow to bend it by hand. Double-click the handle to go back to the automatic route.

### 11. Connect across weeks
![Arrows across weeks](gifs/11-flechas-entre-semanas.gif)

Arrows work between any two elements, however far apart on the canvas — useful for dependencies, follow-ups or "this leads to that".

### 12. Tray for plans without a date
![Tray](gifs/12-bandeja-de-planes.gif)

Ideas that don't have a date yet live in a tray. Drag one onto a day to schedule it, or drag a plan back to the tray to unschedule it. Loose plans can also float directly on the canvas.

## Files & attachments

### 13. Paste or drop anything
![Paste and drop files](gifs/13-pegar-y-soltar-archivos.gif)

Drop images and PDFs, or paste text and links, straight onto the canvas. They become movable, resizable objects. YouTube links can be kept as a titled link or embedded as a video.

### 14. Arrange them freely
![Move objects](gifs/14-mover-objetos.gif)

Objects sit anywhere on the canvas, next to the plans they relate to.

### 15. Attach an object to a plan
![Attach to a plan](gifs/15-adjuntar-a-un-plan.gif)

Drag an object onto a plan and it becomes an attachment of that event. The card shows a clip with the number of attachments.

### 16. View and download attachments
![View and download attachments](gifs/16-ver-y-descargar-adjuntos.gif)

Attachments live with the plan: open them in a viewer and download the original file. PDFs over 15 MB are compressed in the browser before upload.

## Cover images

### 17. Automatic cover search
![Cover image search](gifs/17-buscador-de-portadas.gif)

Type a title and search for a cover image without leaving the editor. It queries Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons and Openverse in parallel (plus Pexels, Pixabay, Unsplash or Google Images if you add a free key), translates Spanish queries to English, ranks results by relevance and offers "load more". Try *"cepillo de dientes"* for a dentist appointment.

### 18. Choose the focal point
![Focal point and result](gifs/18-encuadre-y-resultado.gif)

Drag the image to choose which part stays visible. The cover then shows on the card at every size — as a banner on big cards, as a background on compact ones.

## Organising & finding

### 19. Tag several days at once
![Tag days](gifs/19-etiquetas-en-varios-dias.gif)

Select a range of days and apply a label, colour and note to all of them. A side inspector lists the plans of the selection and lets you add new ones in bulk.

### 20. Filter by person
![Filter by person](gifs/20-filtro-por-persona.gif)

Colour-coded chips with live counters filter the whole canvas by owner (each member, *Todos* or *Externo*), with presets such as *mine*, *everyone else's* or *the group's*.

### 21. Instant search
![Search](gifs/21-busqueda.gif)

`Ctrl + K` opens a command palette that finds any plan and flies the camera straight to it.

### 22. Light and dark themes
![Themes](gifs/22-tema-claro-oscuro.gif)

Dark by default, with a light theme that keeps the same hierarchy and a UI scale that adapts to browser zoom.
